Implications of Edge of Stability

What Edge of Stability changes about how we think about optimization, sharpness, implicit bias, hyperparameters, and practical training dynamics.

Last updated: June 23, 2026

Edge of Stability is not just a curiosity about loss curves. If training routinely operates near an instability boundary, then the optimizer is not merely descending a landscape until it reaches a passive minimum. The algorithm is interacting with curvature, stepsize, stochasticity, and architecture in a way that can shape which regions are reachable and which solutions are selected.

This changes the role of sharpness. Sharpness is not only a post-hoc property of a solution, and it is not always a clean proxy for generalization. It can be part of the training mechanism itself. The implication is that hyperparameters are not just knobs for speed or regularization strength; they may determine the instability boundary around which training self-organizes. This makes Edge of Stability a useful lens for implicit bias, but also a warning against overinterpreting any single curvature threshold.
